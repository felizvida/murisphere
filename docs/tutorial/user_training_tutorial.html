1) Quick Start and Required Setup

Core rule: print the cage card, scan the QR with a phone, open the cage in the browser, and complete the task immediately.

Technician PI / Manager Admin Optional Desktop Companion

1.1 First Login and Dashboard Review

  1. Log in from phone, tablet, browser, or desktop companion.
  2. Review the landing dashboard before entering rooms.
  3. Check alert severity, overdue work, room pressure, and planner risk.
  4. Use clickable cage, project, and lab names to drill in directly.

1.2 Card Printing and Scan Preflight

  1. Open the cage card center.
  2. Set Scan Base URL to a phone-reachable host or domain.
  3. Print at 100% scale.
  4. Use the QR square for phone scans.
  5. Treat the 1D barcode as optional hardware-scanner support only.

1.3 Validate a Real Phone Scan

  1. Use the phone camera, not a separate mobile app.
  2. Scan a printed QR card.
  3. Tap the browser prompt.
  4. Verify cage ID and location before saving any edit.

1.4 Optional Desktop Companion

  1. Use desktop mode for batch printing and exports.
  2. Keep phone and tablet workflows for in-room work.
  3. Do not replace the phone-scan workflow with desktop-only steps.

2) Mouse Technician Daily Workflows

2.1 Start-of-Shift Dashboard Triage

  1. Open the dashboard.
  2. Review high-severity alerts first.
  3. Review overdue tasks and protocol blocks.
  4. Prioritize rooms with dense alert clusters.

2.2 Print or Reprint Cage Cards

  1. Search for target cages.
  2. Batch-print cards from the card center.
  3. Inspect print quality before using the cards in the room.
  4. Confirm the QR is sharp and fully visible.

2.3 Scan Printed Card and Quick-Edit

  1. Scan the printed QR with the phone camera.
  2. Open the cage in the browser.
  3. Verify cage code, room, rack, and breeding status.
  4. Update counts, notes, litter events, or status.

2.4 Census and Reconciliation

  1. Start a census session for the room.
  2. Scan each cage in sequence.
  3. Reconcile observed M/F counts with system totals.
  4. Record mismatch notes before leaving the room.

2.5 Breeding Operations

  1. Create or review breeding pairs.
  2. Record timed mating, plug check, litter, weaning, and transfer events.
  3. Review pair productivity before continuing older breeders.
  4. Retire non-productive pairs when criteria are met.

2.6 Health, Welfare, Mortality, and Euthanasia

  1. Log welfare concerns during rounds.
  2. Open vet cases and treatment schedules.
  3. Record mortality and necropsy requirements.
  4. Record euthanasia method, reason, and disposition.

2.7 Tagging, Samples, and Genotyping Logistics

  1. Add ear tag, chip, tube, or well identifiers.
  2. Create sample records with unique sample codes.
  3. Advance sample status through collection, shipment, receipt, and result.
  4. Verify chain-of-custody history before handoff.

2.8 End-of-Shift Sync Discipline

  1. Confirm queued writes are fully synced.
  2. Close open census sessions and unfinished notes.
  3. Escalate unresolved urgent issues before leaving.

3) Facility Manager Daily Workflows

3.1 Morning Operations Dashboard Review

  1. Review alert counts by severity and category.
  2. Review room density, cage pressure, and open task volume.
  3. Open high-risk cages directly from dashboard links.

3.2 Capacity, Quota, and Chargeback Review

  1. Review facility capacity and room/rack utilization.
  2. Review lab quotas and projected cage load.
  3. Review chargeback and per-diem outputs.
  4. Investigate overloaded rooms or underused allocations.

3.3 Alert Ownership and Dispatch

  1. Review active alerts.
  2. Acknowledge known items with owners.
  3. Dispatch notifications for unresolved urgent work.
  4. Confirm escalation channels are correct.

3.4 Recommendation and Consolidation Workflow

  1. Generate recommendations.
  2. Review each item with cage, lab, and risk context.
  3. Decide: accept, adjust, ignore, or complete.
  4. Track outcomes for operational improvement.

3.5 Planner Scenarios and Supply Risk

  1. Create demand scenarios with constraints.
  2. Attach project-level demand where needed.
  3. Evaluate projected deficits, cage demand, and risk.
  4. Assign action owners for high-risk scenarios.

3.6 Desktop Companion and Office Workflows

  1. Use desktop mode for batch card printing.
  2. Use it for long report reviews and exports.
  3. Keep phone and tablet use for room-floor tasks.

4) Regulatory and Compliance Workflows

  • Monitor protocol expiration and hard-stop blocked edits.
  • Record deviations, CAPA items, and closure state.
  • Track quarantine intake, hold, release, and overdue cases.
  • Track qualification requirements for assigned procedures.
  • Capture attachments and e-signature evidence where required.
  • Use audit history to answer who changed what and when.

5) Research Support Workflows

  • Explore pedigree trees for breeding decisions.
  • Submit genotyping orders and receive provider callbacks.
  • Review Mendelian ratio and genotype-deviation alerts.
  • Review planner scenarios tied to project demand.
  • Export CSV, PDF, and workflow job outputs for downstream systems.

7) End-of-Shift / End-of-Day Checklists

Technician End-of-Shift

  1. Ensure queued offline writes are fully synced.
  2. Confirm all health findings and treatments are documented.
  3. Verify mortality, necropsy, and euthanasia entries are complete.
  4. Close open census or round sessions.

Manager / Admin End-of-Day

  1. Clear or assign owners for all high-severity alerts.
  2. Review unresolved deviations and quarantine holds.
  3. Review planner and recommendation items that remain high risk.
  4. Export required summaries for audit or stakeholder handoff.

8) Troubleshooting and Escalation

  • No scan prompt appears: improve lighting, flatten the card, and try the QR square again.
  • The phone reads only the barcode strip: scan the QR square, not the 1D barcode.
  • Wrong page opens: correct Scan Base URL and reprint the card.
  • QR appears blank or broken: verify the QR asset endpoint and regenerate the card.
  • Save appears queued: reconnect and confirm sync before leaving the room.
  • Protocol hard-stop blocks editing: assign a valid protocol before retrying.
  • Callback or sample mismatch: validate callback token, sample code, and animal mapping.
  • Planner risk remains high: assign an owner and create a corrective action immediately.
