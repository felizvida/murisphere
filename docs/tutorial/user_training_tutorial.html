Murisphere Step-by-Step Tutorial

Audience: Technicians, PIs, and Facility Admins

1. Sign In

Open Murisphere in a browser and log in with your assigned credentials.

  • Technician: routine room operations
  • PI: project and forecasting oversight
  • Admin: configuration, import, and governance

2. Set Phone-Reachable Scan URL

Go to Cages tab and set Scan Base URL.

Example: http://10.0.0.24:8000 or your production domain.

3. Generate and Print Cage Cards

Click Generate + Print to produce cage cards with barcode links.

Allow browser pop-ups so the print dialog opens.

4. Scan in the Animal Room

How to use a phone to scan a printed cage card:

  1. Hold the printed card flat under room lighting.
  2. Open your phone's default Camera app (iPhone Camera or Android Camera).
  3. Point the camera at the printed barcode until a link banner appears.
  4. Tap the banner/link to open Murisphere in your mobile browser.
  5. Confirm the cage ID on screen matches the printed card.
  6. Tap Open Murisphere (login for edits) to move into authenticated edit mode.

If scanning fails: move closer, reduce glare, and verify the printed URL uses your phone-reachable host (not localhost).

5. Perform Cage Updates

In Scan/Edit tab, verify cage identity and update:

  • Male/Female counts
  • Breeding status
  • Notes

Click Save Changes. Updates are logged in audit history.

6. Record Lifecycle Events

Use lifecycle and breeding forms for:

  • Weaning
  • Transfers
  • Timed mating and plug checks
  • Harvest and retirement schedules

7. Use Reports and Imports

In Reports tab:

  • Export CSV/XLSX/PDF
  • Upload genotyping CSV
  • Import onboarding Excel (Admin)

8. Monitor Compliance and Capacity

Use Compliance tab to review protocol expiration alerts and audit history.

Use facility capacity views for room/rack utilization management.

9. Daily Checklist

  1. Print cards if needed.
  2. Perform in-room scan/update rounds.
  3. Review scheduled breeding tasks.
  4. Check protocol and compliance alerts.
  5. Run end-of-day exports if required.
